One building for teaching, making and industry partnership

Design principles
P1 · Show the work

Labs face the Forum through glazed screens, so the teaching is visible to anyone passing through.

P2 · Fabric first

Compact form, high airtightness and fixed shading cut demand before any technology is added.

P3 · Loose fit

Long-span floors and services zones let rooms change use without structural change.

P4 · Honest materials

Exposed timber, recycled brick and terrazzo are durable, low-carbon and tactile.

A riverside site between campus and town

2.1 ha brownfield site between the River Halden and Campus Street.

Opportunities

+ ~6,000 people a day pass the entrance

Constraints

03 · Design concept

From site envelope to a building shaped by daylight

Daylight, airflow and encounter shape the massing. No step adds area.

01

Site envelope

60 × 44 × 18 m: too deep for daylight.

02

Split into two wings

15 m-deep wings: daylight and cross-ventilation.

03

Carve the Forum

14 m glazed atrium: social and ventilation heart.

04

Tune to climate

South wing drops a storey; PV, green roof, timber fins.

15 mWing depth: daylight factor ≥ 3% to 92% of floor area
14 mForum width for stack-effect ventilation
40%Window-to-wall ratio on south and north façades
